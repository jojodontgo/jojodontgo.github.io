Finance Hub

A private budgeting tool for one household

What it is

Finance Hub is a self-hosted tool that two people in one household use to keep their shared budget up to date. It runs on a computer in their home and is reachable only on their own private network. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it does with Gmail

With each person's permission, it reads payment and bill emails from their Gmail, read-only. It looks only at emails from a short list of senders they chose (payment apps, billers and banks). It checks that each email really comes from that sender, picks out the amount, date and payee, and shows them on a private page. Nothing is recorded in the budget until one of them approves it. It cannot send, change or delete email.

Privacy

Email data stays in the household. It is not sold, shared for advertising or used to train AI models. The details are in the privacy policy.
