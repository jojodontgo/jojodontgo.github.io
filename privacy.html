Privacy policy

Finance Hub · last updated 8 November 2026

Who uses it

Finance Hub is a private tool run by one household for its own two members. It is not offered to anyone else. Its only users are the two people whose Gmail accounts it reads, and each of them gives permission separately.

What Google data it accesses

Finance Hub asks for one Google permission: read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly). With it, Finance Hub:

  • searches Gmail for emails from a short list of senders the household chose (payment apps, billers and banks) and downloads only those emails;
  • once, while it is being set up, reads only the sender address of recent payment and bill emails, to count which companies send them, so the household can choose that list.

It cannot send, change, label or delete email, and it does not read other emails.

How it uses that data

  • It checks that each email really comes from the sender it claims (its digital signature), and ignores any that fail.
  • It picks out the amount, date and payee, using fixed rules or a language model that runs on the household's own computer. No Gmail data is sent to an online AI service for this.
  • It shows the result, with the email's text, on a private page that only the two household members can open. Nothing goes into their budget until one of them approves it.

Where it is kept, and for how long

  • On one computer in the household's home, reachable only on their private network.
  • Downloaded emails are deleted after 30 days. Email text shown for approval is removed after 30 days. Approved amounts, dates and payees stay in the household's budget.
  • Backups are encrypted on that computer before being copied to the household's own cloud storage, which therefore holds only encrypted data.

Sharing

Gmail data is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to decide creditworthiness or lending, and not used to train AI models. It is not given to anyone outside the household, with one exception the household controls: to write the fixed rules, the owner may share a few redacted sample emails with an AI coding assistant. Before that, every digit is replaced, email addresses and the household's names are removed, and the owner reads each sample and removes anything else personal.

Finance Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping access

Either person can stop Finance Hub reading their Gmail at any time: in their Google Account, open Security, then third-party connections, and remove Finance Hub. They can also ask the household to delete the emails and items already stored from their account.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions go to the support email shown on Finance Hub's Google sign-in screen.
